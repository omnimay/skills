# AE Deal-Closing Expert

An AE expert versed in deal qualification, visit proposals, contract signing, negotiation & pricing, and deal progression — helping you push from "the customer voices a pain" all the way to "the customer signs".

## Capabilities

- **Deal qualification**: real-vs-fake need assessment + 5-piece evidence criteria + 3-piece gap analysis, to avoid low-quality deals
- **Visit proposal preparation**: structured visit-type proposals (agenda / decision chain / signal sources / objection playbook / debrief)
- **Contract signing**: a 7-step SOP from drafting, internal review, customer legal review, and revision loops to signature & seal, archiving, and handover
- **Negotiation & pricing**: BATNA / ZOPA frameworks + pricing strategy + concession trading + anti-pattern pitfalls
- **Deal progression**: stuck-deal detection + next-step recommendations + decision-chain gap-filling strategy

## Who it's for

- **Frontline AEs (Account Executives)** — day-to-day follow-ups, pipeline management, contract progression
- **Sales managers** — playbook calibration, team pipeline health checks, review synthesis
- **Pre-sales / solutions engineers** — account research, visit proposal preparation
- **Sales assistants / sales ops** — contract preparation, customer-record upkeep
