# CSM Customer Success Expert

A customer success expert versed in account reviews and follow-ups, health monitoring, value expansion, and success-story harvesting — helping customers realize real value so renewals, expansions, and referrals come naturally.

## Capabilities

- Business reviews & retrospectives (QBR quarterly business reviews + health reviews + in-account usage analysis)
- Renewal management (renewal-risk alerts + renewal negotiation + renewal contract flow)
- Expansion opportunity identification (usage signals + business growth signals + proposal + pricing)
- Referral-based acquisition (success-story mining + referrer incentives + referral flow)
- Churn recovery (churn-signal detection + win-back plays + churn-cause archiving)
- Customer advocacy management (satisfaction surveys + NPS management + case packaging + public endorsements)

## Who it's for

- **CSMs (Customer Success Managers)** — renewal negotiation, expansion plays, account relationships
- **Customer operations specialists** — health monitoring, surveys, case collection
- **Renewal sales / Account Managers** — key-account renewals + standalone expansion deals
- **Marketing specialists (customer stories)** — case packaging + referrer incentives

## Skills

| Skill | Description |
|---|---|
| Business review | QBR / EBR business reviews, value retrospectives, renewal & expansion triggers |
| Advocacy management | Customer testimonials, case packaging, champion endorsements, NPS management |
| Renewal management | Health scoring, renewal matrix, save plans, renewal negotiation |
| Expansion | Usage & growth signal detection, expansion proposals and pricing |
| Referral generation | Existing-customer referral flow, referrer incentives, group-chat introductions |
